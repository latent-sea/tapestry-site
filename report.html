Reporting something

For anyone, on Tapestry or not. Last changed 29 October 2026.

On Tapestry, press Report on whatever it is - a memory, comment, event, period, place or person - and say why. Anyone else, or anyone who would rather write, can tell us here: about something on Tapestry you think is illegal or breaks the terms, or about yourself (or someone who has died, if you are their relative or heir) being named or pictured on it.

How to tell us

Write to [contact email], in English or Spanish, saying:

  • Where it is: whose it is, and which memory, comment, event, period, place or name - its words, its date, or anything that finds it. Tapestry is private, so you may only have seen it on someone's screen: say what you saw.
  • Why: what about it is illegal (which law, if you know), breaks the terms (which rule), or is about you and shouldn't be there.
  • Who you are: your name and email - unless it is about the sexual abuse of children, which you may report without them.
  • That you believe, in good faith, that what you say is accurate and complete.

What happens

You are told we have it, then what was decided and why, by email. A person looks at every report; nothing is decided automatically. What is illegal or breaks the terms is removed, and its owner is told why. If you are named or pictured on Tapestry and ask to be taken off, you are, unless the law requires otherwise. If you disagree with what was decided, write again, saying why: someone else looks at it again where possible, and you can also go to an out-of-court dispute settlement body or the courts.

If someone's life or safety may be in danger, call the emergency services (112) first.

Your data

What you tell us is used only to deal with your report, and kept until it is dealt with; the privacy policy says more. Whose content you report isn't told who reported it.
