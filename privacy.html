Privacy

Tapestry's privacy policy. Last changed 28 October 2026.

Tapestry is a place to keep your life's memories, events and periods, and to share them with the people you choose. This page says what Tapestry keeps about you, why, who sees it, and how to have it all deleted.

Who is responsible for your data

The controller of your personal data is [legal name], trading as Latent Sea, NIF [NIF], [postal address], Spain. Write to [contact email] about anything on this page.

What Tapestry keeps

  • Your account: the name and email address of the Google account you sign in with, the name you choose to go by on Tapestry, when you joined and by whose invitation.
  • What you put on it: your memories (their words, pictures, captions and links), events, periods, places and their addresses, labels, comments, the people you name and your circles of them.
  • Your settings: your birthday if you give it (seen by you alone), who sees new things by default, your kinds of period and of event, what cards show, and the people you block.
  • What happens between members: invitations, requests and their answers, notices, and reports of content made to Tapestry's admins.
  • Your plan: whether you are on the free or the paid plan and, if you pay, a reference to the payment from the store you paid through. Tapestry never sees or keeps your card details.
  • On your device only: a few choices kept in your browser, such as text size and the order you read your life in.

Tapestry has no advertising, sells nothing about you, and uses no tracking or analytics services.

Why it is kept

  • To run Tapestry for you - your account, what you put on it, and showing it to whom you choose: because you asked us to (the contract between us, by the terms).
  • To keep Tapestry safe and working - dealing with reports, blocking, keeping a record of who looked at what, and support seeing an account masked: our legitimate interest in a safe, working service, and the law's requirements of us.
  • Support seeing your account as it is: only when you let them, from You, for 24 hours at a time (your consent, which you can stop at any moment).

Who sees it

You decide, on each memory, event and period: only you, the people who were there, all your people, or only those you pick - and you can let in or keep out anyone. The platform enforces this itself, not the app on your phone. Places are seen only through something at them; anyone but you sees only a place's town, region and country, never its street or postcode. Your pictures are kept privately and shown only through short-lived addresses made for someone allowed to see them.

Tapestry's admins can see who is on Tapestry and their email addresses, and the invitations waiting.

Moderators - the admins, and people they choose - see in full, pictures included, a memory or comment someone reported as breaking the terms, while it is reported, so they can decide whether to remove it.

Support (an admin) can see an account as its member sees it, to help with a problem - but with every word jumbled and every picture blacked out, so nothing anyone wrote or pictured can be read. Only if you let them, from You, do they see it as it is, for 24 hours at a time. Neither ever changes anything.

Every time support or a moderator sees something, it is written down: who, what and when. You see in You each time it was something of yours.

Who else handles it

  • Google signs you in. Google's own privacy policy covers your Google account. Google is in the United States, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Latent Sea's own server holds everything on Tapestry - your account, what you put on it, your pictures. It is a server Latent Sea runs itself, rented from netcup GmbH in Nuremberg, Germany, inside the EU; netcup provides the machine and never looks at what is on it.
  • GitHub (in the United States, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework) serves the app's own pages and code - none of your data - and so sees your device's address as you open them.
  • Payments, when you go on the paid plan, are taken by Google Play (in the app) or the website's payment provider (on the web), under their own privacy policies.

Everything travels to and from Tapestry encrypted (HTTPS).

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and everything on it at any time, yourself: sign in, open You, and press Delete my account. Your memories, pictures, events, periods, places, people, comments, notices and settings are deleted straight away. Where others tagged you, you remain only as a name they typed.

You can also ask for your account to be deleted by writing to Latent Sea at [contact email] from the email address you sign in with.

Before deleting, you can download a copy of everything in your life from You, with Keep a copy.

Your rights

You may ask to see the data Tapestry keeps about you, to have it corrected, deleted, limited or handed to you to take elsewhere, and object to how it is used - by writing to [contact email]. Much of it you can do yourself: change anything in Tapestry, download everything with Keep a copy, or delete your account. If you are unhappy with how your data is handled, you may complain to the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).

How long it is kept

For as long as your account is open. Deleted, it is gone straight away. The record of who looked at what is kept for as long as the account it is about.

Children

Tapestry is not for children under 14.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too, and anything important is said in Tapestry. Questions go to [contact email].
