Privacy

Tapestry's privacy policy. Last changed 28 October 2026.

Tapestry is a place to keep your life's memories, events and periods, and to share them with the people you choose. It is made and run by Latent Sea. This page says what Tapestry keeps about you, who sees it, and how to have it all deleted.

What Tapestry keeps

  • Your account: the name and email address of the Google account you sign in with, the name you choose to go by on Tapestry, when you joined and by whose invitation.
  • What you put on it: your memories (their words, pictures, captions and links), events, periods, places and their addresses, labels, comments, the people you name and your circles of them.
  • Your settings: your birthday if you give it (seen by you alone), who sees new things by default, your kinds of period and of event, what cards show, and the people you block.
  • What happens between members: invitations, requests and their answers, notices, and reports of content made to Tapestry's admins.
  • Your plan: whether you are on the free or the paid plan and, if you pay, a reference to the payment from the store you paid through. Tapestry never sees or keeps your card details.
  • On your device only: a few choices kept in your browser, such as text size and the order you read your thread in.

Tapestry has no advertising, sells nothing about you, and uses no tracking or analytics services.

Who sees it

You decide, on each memory, event and period: only you, the people who were there, all your people, or only those you pick - and you can let in or keep out anyone. The platform enforces this itself, not the app on your phone. Places are seen only through something at them; anyone but you sees only a place's town, region and country, never its street or postcode. Your pictures are kept privately and shown only through short-lived addresses made for someone allowed to see them.

Tapestry's admins can see who is on Tapestry and their email addresses, the invitations waiting, and content reported to them. They see nobody's memories other than as any member would.

Who else handles it

  • Google signs you in. Google's own privacy policy covers your Google account.
  • Latent Sea's own server holds everything on Tapestry. It is not shared with anyone else.
  • Payments, when you go on the paid plan, are taken by Google Play (in the app) or the website's payment provider (on the web), under their own privacy policies.

Everything travels to and from Tapestry encrypted (HTTPS).

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and everything on it at any time, yourself: sign in, open You, and press Delete my account. Your memories, pictures, events, periods, places, people, comments, notices and settings are deleted straight away. Where others tagged you, you remain only as a name they typed.

You can also ask for your account to be deleted by writing to Latent Sea at [contact email] from the email address you sign in with.

Before deleting, you can download a copy of everything on your thread from You, with Keep a copy.

Children

Tapestry is not for children under 13.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too, and anything important is said in Tapestry. Questions go to [contact email].
